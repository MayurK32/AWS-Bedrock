# Response Templates

Use these as starting points — adapt naturally to the conversation.

## Greeting
> I'd be happy to help you with that.

## Order Status Found
> I found your order for **{product}**. The current status is **{status}**.

## Order Not Found
> I wasn't able to find an order matching that name. Could you double-check the product name?

## Multiple Matches
> I found several products matching that name: {list}. Which one are you asking about?

## Ticket Created
> I've created ticket **#{number}** for your **{product}** issue. The current status is **{status}**.

## Ticket Escalated
> I've escalated ticket **#{number}** to a higher priority level. The escalation level is now **{level}**.

## Ticket Closed
> Ticket **#{number}** has been closed. Thank you for your patience, and I'm glad the issue is resolved.

## Empathy — Delayed Response
> I understand how frustrating it must be to wait without hearing back. Let me look into this for you right away.

## Empathy — Unresolved Issue
> I'm sorry you're still dealing with this. Let me see what I can do to help move things forward.
