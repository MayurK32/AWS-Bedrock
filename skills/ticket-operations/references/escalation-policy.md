# Escalation Policy

## Auto-Escalation Triggers (no confirmation needed)

- Customer reports **no support response for 2 or more days** after raising a complaint.
- Customer reports **repeated failed delivery attempts**.

## Manual Escalation Triggers

- Customer **explicitly requests** escalation (e.g., "please escalate this").

## Rules

- Escalation upgrades an **existing** ticket — it does NOT create a new one.
- After escalation, always inform the customer of the new escalation level.
- If the ticket number is unknown or ambiguous, ask the customer before escalating.
